Join Us

We are always glad to hear from students and researchers who are curious about working at the intersection of machine learning and the life sciences.

Research areas

  • Deep learning for protein and RNA 3D structure prediction and refinement
  • AI-ready RNA motif data and graph learning for RNA structure
  • Quality assessment of predicted molecular structures
  • LLM-based AI agents that automate structural-bioinformatics workflows
  • Machine learning for clinical decision support (anticoagulant dosing, EHR data)
  • Research security and scientific integrity in AI-driven biology

Helpful background

  • Programming experience (Python preferred) and comfort with Linux/HPC environments
  • Background in one or more of: machine learning, statistics, computational biology, structural biology, or applied mathematics
  • Curiosity about biology and willingness to learn across disciplines
  • Undergraduates: no prior research experience required — enthusiasm and consistency matter most

Getting in touch

Prospective Ph.D. students interested in our research can apply to the CMSE graduate program at MSU and indicate an interest in working with Dr. Hou in the application; admission decisions are made through the graduate program. If you would like to introduce yourself, please send a short note on your research interests along with your CV, using the subject line “Prospective PhD student – Hou Lab”.

Current MSU students (graduate or undergraduate) interested in research experience: feel free to email Dr. Hou with your CV and a brief note on which of the areas above interest you.

Contact: houjie@msu.edu
