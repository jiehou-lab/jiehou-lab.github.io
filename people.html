Principal Investigator

Jie Hou, Ph.D.

Assistant Professor, Department of Computational Mathematics, Science and Engineering (CMSE), Michigan State University

Dr. Hou is a tenure-track Assistant Professor in the Department of CMSE at Michigan State University (since January 2026). He was previously an Associate Professor with tenure in the Department of Computer Science at Saint Louis University, where he founded the lab in 2019 and held a secondary appointment in the Department of Health and Clinical Outcomes Research, School of Medicine. He received his M.A. in Statistics (2014) and Ph.D. in Computer Science (2019) from the University of Missouri–Columbia, working with Dr. Jianlin Cheng.

His research develops machine learning, deep learning, and optimization methods for protein/RNA structure prediction, molecular interactions, and structure quality assessment, with collaborative work in healthcare analytics, privacy-preserving ML, and omics data analysis.

Email: houjie@msu.edu
