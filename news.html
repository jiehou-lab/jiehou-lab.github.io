News

2026

  • Aug 2026
    FundingDr. Hou received a new NSF EAGER award, “Collaborative Research: EAGER: Unveiling Security Risks to Scientific Integrity in AI-Driven Biological Discovery” (Award 2628291). The project maps how externally sourced datasets, reference databases, and pretrained models enter AI-driven biological workflows, and measures how integrity failures in those resources propagate to scientific conclusions. See Grants.
  • Jun 2026
    FundingDr. Hou (co-PI) and Dr. Si Chen (PI, School of Planning, Design and Construction) received an MSU AI Ready Spartans Innovation Grant for “Urban AI: AI-Driven Decision Support for Real-World Urban Challenges” — a 20-hour online, non-credit learning experience open to MSU students from all majors. Course page.
  • Mar 2026
    PosterUndergraduate researchers Rahul Jasthi, Vivek Shenoy, Ahmed Bektic, and Ellen Nguyen presented “AutoRNA: Incorporating a Fully Automated AI Agent into Various Advanced RNA Structural Analyses” at the Sigma Xi 2026 Research Symposium at Saint Louis University (March 30). AutoRNA is now part of the ARSMA platform with the full CoSSMos workflow and a structured multi-agent governance loop. Photo & details.
  • Jan 2026
    Lab newsDr. Hou joined the Department of Computational Mathematics, Science and Engineering (CMSE) at Michigan State University as a tenure-track Assistant Professor. The Hou Lab is now based in East Lansing and is recruiting.
